Project Mission

A group assignment exploring Algorithmic Problem Solving through immersive multimedia.

Algorithmic Problem Solving is a core subject in computer programming. Our platform uses the Gagné’s Nine Events of Instruction to support beginners in learning through a structured, interactive, and self-paced approach. We aim to bridge the gap between abstract logic and real C++ implementation.
