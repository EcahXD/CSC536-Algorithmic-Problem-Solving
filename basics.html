Foundations of Problem Solving

Level 01: Mastering the Logic Before the Code

Why Learn Algorithms?

Every software, app, and AI starts as an algorithm. It is the logical blueprint of a program. Before you touch a keyboard, you must master the art of breaking a complex problem into simple, executable instructions.

Key Fact: An algorithm is language-independent. You can write an algorithm and implement it in C++, Python, or Java!

The Analysis Phase: How to Start?

In CSC402, problem-solving starts with the "Analysis" step of the PDLC. We use the IPO Model to break down a problem into three core components.

1
Inputs

What data do we need from the user?
e.g., Radius of a circle, User's age.

2
Processes

What formulas or logic are needed?
e.g., $$Area = 3.14159 \times r^2$$

3
Outputs

What information must be displayed?
e.g., Display "The Area is...".
